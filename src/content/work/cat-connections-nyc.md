problem: >
  Cat Connections NYC, a small Astoria-based cat rescue, needed a real digital
  home - a site that could showcase adoptable and adopted cats, tell the
  organization's story, and actually convert visitors into donors and
  volunteers, not just describe the mission.
role: >
  I served as the organization's technical lead, fundraising
  director, and social media manager, owning the website end to end alongside branding,
  social media, and fundraising strategy. This was full digital ownership,
  not a single deliverable: design, CMS build, campaign execution, and
  public-facing representation all sat with me.
decisions:
  - "Built the site on Webflow CMS with a structured content model so adoptable/adopted cats, campaigns, and impact stories could be updated without a developer."
  - "Ran fundraising across multiple platforms at once - Raisely, Zeffy, GoFundMe, Reddit, Instagram, and Facebook - rather than relying on a single channel."
  - "Used Reddit specifically for community-driven fundraising posts, a channel most nonprofit sites ignore entirely."
result: "$18,000 raised in 5 months across a multi-platform campaign, including a Fidelity Corporate Giving match."
