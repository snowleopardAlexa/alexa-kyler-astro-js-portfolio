overview: |
  Wildari Planet is an independent wildlife conservation and education platform I designed and built entirely from scratch. The goal was to create a content-rich, fast, accessible site that teaches people about animal species, conservation status, and wildlife science. Its design is modern and user-friendly - something animal education websites often lack.
built:
  - "Designed the UX/UI, starting from wireframes in Figma, to deliver the polished experience many science-heavy animal websites lack - a modern layout and a distinct visual identity that encourage people to learn about animals."
  - "Built interactive quizzes in different difficulty tiers so visitors can check their knowledge as they learn."
  - "Set up Drupal 10 on my local machine and architected it as a headless CMS, with custom content types for animal species profiles, conservation articles, and educational resources."
  - "Built Drupal taxonomies for continents and animal types, and a filter system that lets visitors browse animals by continent and conservation status."
  - "Created back-end user roles in Drupal so different people get the right level of access to manage content."
  - "Built the Astro.js front end consuming Drupal's JSON:API - delivering fast, JS-light pages with excellent Core Web Vitals scores."
  - "Used Claude Code as an AI coding assistant throughout the build - accelerating component development, debugging, and documentation while maintaining human review of all generated code."
  - "Configured Cloudflare Workers for hosting and deployment, with Git/GitHub for version control."
  - "Implemented WCAG accessibility standards, mobile-first responsive design, and semantic HTML throughout."
