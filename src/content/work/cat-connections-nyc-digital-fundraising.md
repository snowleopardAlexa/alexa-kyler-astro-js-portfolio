overview: |
  With no existing donor base and no budget, I built Cat Connections NYC's donation forms in Raisely and the campaigns that brought donors to them. Every form was designed around a single cat's story, so supporters knew exactly who they were helping. The result was $18,000+ raised in under six months, funding medical care for cats including cancer treatment, corrective eye surgery, and emergency procedures.
built:

  - "Made storytelling the core of each form - working closely with our fosters and trappers, interviewing them to capture every detail of a cat's rescue and medical needs, and writing that story into the campaign"
  - "Promoted each campaign across social media - Reddit, Facebook groups, and Instagram - to reach donors beyond our immediate community"
  - "Linked every campaign in our Instagram Linktree so supporters could easily find a campaign and donate to a specific cat"
  - "Created GoFundMe donation pages for individual cats' medical fundraising, before we started using Raisely"
  - "Raised funds with Meta's and Instagram's native fundraising tools alongside the Raisely forms"
