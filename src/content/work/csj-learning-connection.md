problem: >
  CSJ Learning Connection is a program of the Sisters of St. Joseph that has served its community for more than 30 years, helping immigrant women learn English. Despite decades of real achievements, the program had no online presence at all. The goal was to build a beautiful, fully responsive website on Webflow that would finally show its mission to the world.
role: >
  I was the web designer and developer, building the site structure, visual
  direction, and content, and recommending logo updates. Beyond the build,
  I advised the team directly on fundraising technology.
built:
  - "Designed and built a fully responsive website on Webflow, following UX/UI best practices"
  - "Grounded the design in community research and tested the prototype with the program's donors, students, and teachers"
  - "Built a language switcher with custom JavaScript so visitors can read the site in either language"
  - "Wrote custom JavaScript to improve performance and boost the site's Google PageSpeed score"
  - "Implemented Raisely as the program's fundraising software, then designed and built its donation page and form in Raisely"
  - "Created case studies in Canva documenting the project and shared them with the program's director"
